Projects

Scientific Discovery

In 2024, I received a Monograph Grant from the Carlsberg Foundation for the book project Scientific Discovery: Its Nature, Social Function, and Future, to be conducted in the academic year 2024–25.

Intuitions in Science and Philosophy

In 2015, I received a Sapere Aude Starting Grant from the Danish Council for Independent Research for a four-year project on Intuitions in Science and Philosophy. The project started in January 2016 and ended in December 2019. The project team consisted of me, three postdocs and one PhD student.

The project investigated whether and how intuitive judgements can have an evidential function in philosophy, linguistics, and fundamental science, with the aim of better understanding methodological similarities across these fields.

Towards a New Epistemology of Science

In 2010 I received an Emmy-Noether research grant from the German Research Foundation (DFG). This project investigated the ways in which theoretical presuppositions can influence the assessment of data, including both negative theoretical bias and potentially productive theory-driven data reliability judgements.
