Teaching

At Aarhus University I teach Philosophy of Science introduction courses to students at the Faculty of Natural Science.

  • Philosophy of Physics, Chemistry, and Geology
  • Philosophy and Ethics of Science: Biology, Molecular Biology and Agrobiology
  • Philosophy and Ethics of Science: Physics and Geoscience

The Centre for Science Studies also offers a Master’s programme. Courses include:

  • Advanced Topics in the Philosophy of Science
  • History and Philosophy of Science and Technology
